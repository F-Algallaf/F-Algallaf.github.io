04 — Projects

Selected work.

Four projects spanning public-sector analytics, operations forecasting, commercial BI, and credit risk.

01

Chicago Crimes & Socioeconomic Analysis

Investigated three years of Chicago crime records (2023–2025) alongside ACS five-year socioeconomic data to understand how income, age, and demographic patterns shape crime distribution across community areas. After extensive cleaning and feature engineering — including temporal and socioeconomic indicators — I clustered community areas with K-Means and Agglomerative Clustering to surface distinct neighborhood profiles, then built Random Forest, XGBoost, and Logistic Regression models to classify violent versus non-violent crime.

Python Pandas Scikit-Learn XGBoost Matplotlib Seaborn
02

BTS Flight Delays & Diversion Prediction

Mined multi-year BTS operations and airline datasets to identify the operational drivers behind flight delays and diversions. To handle heavily skewed delay data and outliers, I benchmarked Ordinary Least Squares, Weighted Least Squares, and Logistic Regression against one another — isolating the most robust approach and producing diversion forecasts intended to support proactive operational planning.

Python R Scikit-Learn
03

Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

Designed a suite of five interactive Tableau dashboards translating four years of sales data (2020–2023) into a clear strategic narrative for senior management. The analysis combined customer segmentation, discount-to-profit sensitivity testing, and profitability forecasting to pinpoint exactly which segments and discount structures were eroding margin — and which were driving it.

Tableau
04

Credit Risk Analysis

Built a loan-approval prediction pipeline from applicants' demographic and financial data, then put Bagging and Random Forest classifiers head-to-head across accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. The comparison surfaces which ensemble strategy offers the more reliable foundation for real-world credit decisioning.

Python Machine Learning
